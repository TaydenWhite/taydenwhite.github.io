I'm a senior at Purdue University pursuing dual degrees in **Computer Science** and **Mathematical Economics**, graduating in May 2027.

I've been writing code since I learned Python in 8th grade. Back then, a question about the stock market or some other financial puzzle was usually the inspiration, and code was how I explored it.

Today I build LLM pipelines for behavioral research in Purdue's [Communication and Cognition Lab](/research/llm-transcript-coding/), working on [fusion rules for topological quantum computing](/research/permutation-defects/) in the Department of Mathematics, and, most recently, optimizing data systems and designing an [agentic text-to-SQL workflow](/projects/jnj-compliance-dashboards/) as a software engineering co-op at Johnson & Johnson.

Studying two fields that approach problems very differently has given me a well-rounded skill set, and it has led me to a wide range of opportunities, from research labs to industry teams.

I'm graduating in May 2027 and looking for full-time roles in **software engineering**, **machine learning**, and **research**, starting summer 2027. The best way to reach me is [email](mailto:tayden.white@gmail.com).
