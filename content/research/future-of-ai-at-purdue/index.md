## Overview

In the fall of my freshman year, I set out to understand how Purdue's leaders think about generative AI in the classroom, and how that compares with other universities.

## Approach

- **Surveyed Purdue administrators** on classroom AI policy.
- **Benchmarked their sentiment against peer institutions**, building a model of “AI-friendliness” to quantify where Purdue diverges from its peers.
- **Analyzed and visualized the results** in Python with pandas and matplotlib.

## Outcome

- Wrote a research paper that visualizes the findings and recommends a path forward for Purdue.
- Presented the findings to 60+ faculty and students at Purdue's Undergraduate Research Exposition.
